Sector Atlas

Sectors and market-cap labels come from your file. Sub-sectors were assigned by hand from each company's main business, following the NSE and SEBI industry classification as a guide. Companies that could not be placed with confidence are listed under Others / Unclassified. Ticker chips open the company page on Screener. The TradeLyf view ranks only the 1,100 most actively traded stocks (by recent traded value), so thinly traded stocks do not distort its list; every other page uses all stocks. Returns are price-only, split-adjusted, from end-of-day closes (hover a chip for all six periods). Top gainers follow the sector and market-cap filters. Sector and sub-sector figures are the median stock return, so a few extreme movers do not skew them. Strength, status and ratings are worked out in your browser from these returns: they rank each group or stock against the rest for the chosen time frame. They are a research aid, not investment advice.
