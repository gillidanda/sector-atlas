Sector Atlas

Sectors and market-cap labels come from your file. Sub-sectors were assigned by hand from each company's main business, following the NSE and SEBI industry classification as a guide. Companies that could not be placed with confidence are listed under Others / Unclassified. Ticker chips open the company page on Screener. Returns are price-only, split-adjusted, from end-of-day closes (hover a chip for all four periods). Top gainers follow the sector and market-cap filters. Sector and sub-sector figures are the median stock return, so a few extreme movers do not skew them.
